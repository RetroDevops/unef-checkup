# Privacy

Unef Checkup is designed to collect nothing.

## What it reads

- Whether Private DNS is currently on, and its server name if so.
- The list of installed apps and the permissions each one requests, to produce
  the on-screen risk check.

## What it stores

On the device only, in the app's private storage:

- a flag that you have seen the welcome screen, and
- the plain text of your most recent check, so it can be shown again when you
  reopen the app.

## What it sends

Nothing. The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
The "Send this report to my family" button hands the text to Android's share
sheet, where you choose whether and to whom to send it.

## What it changes

Nothing on its own. Actions such as enabling DNS blocking, uninstalling an app,
clearing browser data, or backing up happen on Android's own settings screens,
which you open from this app and confirm yourself.
